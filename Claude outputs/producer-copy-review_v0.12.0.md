# Cape Wine Atlas — producer copy as published (v0.12.0)

One entry per producer: the history line and the people line the site shows, both derived from the atlas record. Blank means the record holds nothing publishable yet. Mark anything wrong or out of place and it can be corrected at the source.

## bot-river

**Beaumont Family Wines**  
Compagnes Drift: 1700s Dutch East India Company supply farm (fruit, dairy, wine, flour) holding the ward's oldest wine cellar; wine made in the 1940s, lapsed late 1960s.  
People: Beaumont — Raoul & Jayne (1974); Sebastian & Nici running since 2015; daughter Ariane.  

**Gabriëlskloof Wine Estate**  
The ward's destination hub 'on the koppie' beside the N2.  
People: Heyns family (Bernhard Heyns, founder-owner); winemaker Peter-Allan Finlayson (son-in-law) with wife Nicolene Heyns-Finlayson.  

**Luddite Wines**  
Founded 1999 with one aim — world-class Shiraz — while Niels was still Beaumont's winemaker (the ward's own talent-tree edge). Among the first to plant Shiraz in Bot River; first vintage 2000.  
People: Niels & Penny Verburg; daughter Alice in the cellar since 2017.  

**Wildekrans Wine Estate**  
1,000ha estate among rhenosterbos, 35ha under vine; founded 1970s as mixed farming (wheat, olives, ostriches) before the cool terroir won.  

**Barton Vineyards**  
~200ha (500-acre) farm bordering the Kogelberg reserve, 33ha of vines on cool southerly slopes. Five Tuscan-style villas and a cottage, each with an eco wood-fired hot tub. Frost-free, Walker Bay breezes.  

**Villion Wines**  
Kobie Viljoen is the 'Kobie' whose great work Peter-Allan Finlayson pledged to build on in the 2014 Gabriëlskloof handover — he left Gabriëlskloof that year (after Spier and others, 20+ years in) to found Villion.  
People: Kobie & Elnette Viljoen and their children.  

**Genevieve MCC**  
SA's rare MCC-only house: vintage Cap Classique every year since 2008 (first release 2010). The sheets also place the MCC cellar in Paarl while the vineyards are Bot River.  
People: Melissa Genevieve Nelsen (with partner Leon)  

**Arcangeli Family Vineyards**  
Arcangeli is the former Feiteiras vineyards. Alessandro Arcangeli bought the 16ha farm beside the N2 in 2015; winemaker Krige Visser (of Mount Abora); 'Vini Autentici' minimal-intervention styling, Italian wine names.  
People: Arcangeli family — Alessandro 'Sandro' Arcangeli (first-generation from Rome)  

**Rivendell Estate & Vineyards**  
Austrian-south african estate: founded by Heimo and Maria, whose family's vineyard and distilling heritage traces to Austria — maiden vintages 2011 (Sauvignon Blanc, Shiraz), a dry Shiraz Rosé added since.  
People: Founded by Heimo & Maria (Austrian family — vineyard and distilling heritage in Austria); viticulturist Schalk du Toit.  

**Paardenkloof Estate**  
Boutique single-vineyard estate in a hidden valley; among the pioneers of reintroducing indigenous Nguni cattle to the Western Cape — regenerative-farming heritage central to the brand.  

**Anysbos**  
Johan and Sue Heyns bought the land in 2007; olive oil, cheese and wine 'with patience and care'; first wines ~three years before the guide.  

**Eerste Hoop Wines**  
Mixed farm: olives, goat's-milk cheeses, grapes.  

**Benguela Cove Lagoon Wine Estate**  
200ha lagoon-side estate, 70ha vines + 10ha olive groves, on the Bot River Lagoon at the gateway to Walker Bay.  
People: Penny Streeter OBE (British entrepreneur; owner since 2013)  

**Crystallum**  
People: Andrew and Peter-Allan Finlayson, brothers and the third generation of Finlayson winemakers; Peter-Allan is the winemaker, Andrew the viticulturist.  

**Momento Wines**  
People: Marelise Niemann, winemaker and owner.

## constantia

**Groot Constantia**  
Founded 1685 by Simon van der Stel — the oldest continuously producing wine farm in South Africa; owned by the Groot Constantia Trust (non-profit) to preserve the estate.  

**Klein Constantia Estate**  
Part of Van der Stel's original 1685 Constantia grant (~900ha), which split into Klein Constantia, Groot Constantia, Buitenverwachting and Constantia Uitsig.  

**Steenberg Vineyards**  
Catharina flagship red; global Sauvignon Blanc benchmark reputation; Tryn and Bistro Sixteen82 restaurants, five-star hotel, spa, golf.  

**Buitenverwachting**  
Beyond Restaurant is chef Peter Tempelhoff's winelands venture; Coffee BloC deli. Famous flinty Chardonnay.  

**Constantia Glen**  
Glen Alpine Farm just below Constantia Nek; boutique cool-climate estate; cellarmaster Justin van Wyk. Waibel family (Austrian) ownership signalled by the estate's own Vinothek in Dornbirn, Austria — a dedicated European arm.  

**Beau Constantia**  
No cellar/vineyard tours; age limit applies; parking scarce — producer recommends taxis. this is an ownership relation and nothing more.  

**Eagles' Nest Wines**  
Old Constantia Main Road; winemaker Craig Barnard. Small mountain-slope property rebuilt after the 2000 fires.  

**Silvermist Organic Wine Estate**  
The only certified-organic vineyards on the Constantia Wine Route. Smallest vines on the route: ~6ha, 70% white/30% red, inside Table Mountain National Park, 120-acre property above Constantia Nek.  

**High Constantia Wine Cellar**  
David van Niekerk's one-man resurrection of a historic name: the land dates to 1693 as Wittebomen ('white trees', for its silverleaf trees) under the Dutch East India Company.  

**Constantia Royale (Nova Zonnestraal)**  
Don's Reserve superior blend. Wine is stored and tasted in a tunnel beneath the M3.  

**St Leger Wines**  
Small Constantia winery at Le Marais with an on-site Muscat de Frontignan clos and a subterranean winery.

## durbanville

**Diemersdal Wine Estate**  
First estate wine under own label: Private Collection. No fact about either farm transfers to the other — in particular the fruit is Lambert's Bay's, not Durbanville's.  
People: Louw family, farming here since 1885; Thys Louw is the sixth generation.  

**De Grendel Wine Estate**  
Cape farm dated 1720, Graaff family for five generations; the 3rd Baronet Sir David Graaff established the vineyards and cellar, continued by Sir De Villiers Graaff; cellarmaster Charles Hopkins. ~330ha on Tygerberg Hill.  
People: Graaff family since the 1890s; Sir David Graaff replanted vines in 1999.  

**Durbanville Hills Winery**  
_(no history line yet)_  

**Nitida Wine Farm**  
Veller family bought the smallest Durbanville farm in 1990; engineer Bernhard Veller planted the first vines in 1992 intending 'a few barrels'; first Sauvignon Blanc gold within three years; benchmark Semillon recognition by 2001.  
People: Bernhard and Peta Veller; land purchased 1990, first vines 1992.  

**Klein Roosboom Boutique Winery**  
300-year-old boutique farm; six themed tasting caves knocked into decommissioned concrete fermentation tanks (some still bearing tartaric-acid crystals and old wine-level lines) — the valley's signature quirky venue.  

**Bloemendal Wine Estate**  
First cellar 1920 (Jackie Coetzee, the current family's grandfather); registered as an estate 1987; the 'water lily label'. Winemaker Francois Haasbroek (winefundi spells 'Hassbroek'). Part of 'Die Kloof' vineyards area.  

**Groot Phesantekraal**  
Working family farm dated 1698, run by fourth-generation André and Ronelle Brink; restaurant in the restored 1767 thatched stable (original stone floor and wooden mangers).  

**Maastricht Wines**  
Granted 9 February 1702 to Hendrick Seeger (VOC settler); named by Dutch settlers for soils that reminded them of home. Walk-ins fine; arrives-early advice for busy weekends.  

**Meerendal Wine Estate**  
Granted 1702 to dock master Jan Meerland by W.A. van der Stel; his widow Christina 'Stijntie' Stans doubled the farm by 1712; first vines 1714 (60,000).  

**D'Aria Winery**  
First wine (Sauvignon Blanc) November 2005; 400-ton cellar, hand-picked and hand-sorted; winemaker Elizabeth Meyer. Songbird Reserve SB; Pop Song and Love Song sparklings.  

**Altydgedacht Wine Estate**  
312-year winemaking history (1698) — one of the oldest continuously used cellars in South Africa (the 300-year-old barrel room still in service); Parker family stewardship since 1852, six generations.  
People: Parker family since 1852 (George Francis Parker, arrived at the Cape 1819 with family later settling in Australia).  

**Hillcrest Estate**  
Wine and olive estate on the M13; vineyards 240-400m on the Tygerberg (SB at the summit).  

**Signal Gun Wines**  
Hooggelegen Farm, Vissershok Road — De Wit family for five-plus generations, among the oldest family farms in the valley; M.J. de Wit began making wine as a hobby in 2006, and with wife Estani took over farm management in 2010.  

**Canto Boutique Wines**  
Cap Classique specialist — signature MCC-and-macaron pairing, plus a non-alcoholic macaron/artisan-tonic pairing; old low-yielding dryland vines feed the wooded Chardonnay, Merlot and MCCs. Chapel-like Cape Dutch tasting room.  


## elgin

**Paul Cluver Family Wines**  
The pioneer, precisely dated: Dr Paul Cluver planted Elgin's first commercial vineyards in 1986. Family on De Rust since 1896; 72ha of vines within a holistic farm of apples, pears and a Hereford stud.  
People: Clüver family since 1896 — 4th generation; Paul Clüver Jr MD; cellarmaster Andries Burger.  

**Iona Vineyards**  
420m plateau, ocean 3km, surrounded by 3,000ha of Kogelberg biosphere. Organic/biodynamic farming with a resident flock of geese as pest control; 15ha of apples remain. Winemaker history includes John Seccombe.  

**Oak Valley Estate**  
1898 farm; elgin's first plantings were its 1985 experimental vineyards (commercial primacy stays with Cluver, 1986 — both facts, correctly split); modern wine label from ~2003.  

**Almenkerk Wine Estate**  
Founded 2004: Joris and Natalie van Almenkerk — the Low Countries thread in Elgin, matching Spioenkop's Belgian ownership.  
People: Joris & Natalie van Almenkerk; winemaking Joris van Almenkerk with Cameron Corney (since 2022); viticulturist Michael Keown (since 2014)  

**Richard Kershaw Wines**  
Award/score leads: Decanter 97 (Syrah Clonal Selection 2017, Matt Walls), Wine Advocate 93+ (2016).  
People: Richard Kershaw MW (Sheffield-born)  

**Spioenkop Wines**  
Dense planting, spontaneous ferments only, uncertified organic/biodynamic; the contrarian pairing of Riesling (among SA's best, built to age) and bushvine Pinotage in Sauvignon country.  
People: Koen Roose (Belgian ex-sommelier & engineer; wife Nathalie; partners incl. Jacques, ex-McKinsey)  

**Catherine Marshall Wines**  
Venue-split, reversed: where Shannon grows in Elgin and vinifies in the Hemel-en-Aarde, Catherine Marshall is an Elgin-fruit brand whose cellar door sits in stellenbosch — the Lavinia property on Polkadraai Road.  
People: Catherine Marshall (founder, Cellar Master); son Jono Oxenham (Winemaker)  

**Elgin Vintners**  
Guiding winemaker/viticulturist for years was paul Wallace — who now runs his own label listed separately by the association; current winemaker & business manager is Burgundy-trained Augustus 'Gus' Dale.  

**Highlands Road Estate**  
Estate tasting room at 75 Highlands Road. Sits along the same road as Spioenkop (76) and, 16.5km on, Iona.  

**Charles Fox Cap Classique**  
Elgin's Méthode Cap Classique specialist — the district's cool climate played as a Champagne analogue.  

**South Hill Vineyards**

**Radford Dale Organic (ex-Elgin Ridge)**

**Babylonstoren**  
_(no history line yet)_  

**Bellingham**  
Bellingham (DGB) tastes at Franschhoek Cellar in the village. Bellingham + Franschhoek Cellar + Old Road Wine Co. — three DGB brands, one village cellar-door footprint.  

**Black Elephant Vintners**  
Irreverent boutique brand ('reinventing the art of winemaking… an industry that has long forgotten to have fun'); tasting venue in the village.  

**Boekenhoutskloof Winery**  
Land deeded 1776 in the valley's most secluded corner, named for the Cape beech (boekenhout); relaunched 1993 by seven partners — the seven chairs on the label — with Elsenburg-trained Marc Kent.  
People: Marc Kent (winemaker-partner since 1993) with founding partners; late Tim Rands (Vinimark) long-time Managing Partner.  

**Boschendal Wines**  
Founded 1685 — one of SA's oldest estates: Jean le Long's grant, Nicolas de Lanoy's first vines, then the de Villiers family for over a century, who raised the Cape Dutch manor (National Heritage Monument).  

**Chamonix Wines**  
Part of the 1688 Huguenot grant La Cotte; renamed from 'Waterval' by the Pickering family (1965) for the French alpine village; bought early 1990s by Chris Hellinger as an export-fruit farm and steadily replanted.  
People: Chris Hellinger (German-born entrepreneur; owner since the early 1990s)  

**Colmant Cap Classique & Champagne**  
Chardonnay and Pinot Noir in roughly equal proportions; ~3,300 cases. Guests witness the bottling/filling line on tours.  
People: Colmant family (Belgian) — Paul Colmant hosts.  

**Dieu Donné Vineyards**  
Boutique hillside winery above the village (Uitkyk Street) with panoramic Drakenstein views; small-batch estate wines; intimate tastings.  

**Eikehof**  
The oldest white vineyard in south africa: a 1902 block of Semillon bushvines, still producing — Old Vine Project member, bottled as a numbered rarity (780 bottles one vintage, 550 another.  
People: Malherbe family since 1903 — Francois (4th-generation winemaker) & Elize (tasting room)  

**Franschhoek Cellar**  
Village cellar door (DGB); 'Village Walk' tastings. Bellingham + Franschhoek Cellar + Old Road Wine Co. — three DGB brands, one village cellar-door footprint.  

**Glenwood**  
Supplies Haut Espoir's Semillon/Chardonnay (block DP1).  
People: Alastair Wood (owner, hosts tastings himself); DP Burger — GM & Cellar Master, 30 years, fourth-generation Franschhoek family.  

**The House of GM&Ahrens**  
Boutique Cap Classique makers Gerrit Maritz (founder) and Albert Ahrens.  
People: Gerrit Maritz (founding partner) & Albert Ahrens (co-founding partner); wife Ingrid.  

**Grande Provence Heritage Wine Estate**  
47 acres (~19ha) of vines. Wine-blending experiences for up to 60 guests.  

**Haut Espoir**  
Only 8ha of vines (five Bordeaux/Rhône reds); the rest fynbos restoration, riverine ecosystem, olives, vegetable garden.  
People: Armstrong family (since 1999 — parents, kids and extended family all involved)  

**Haute Cabrière Wine Farm**  
Second-generation Cellar Master Takuan von Arnim; Achim's own art fills cellar and restaurant. wineOrigin: the Pierre Jourdan Brut is labelled WO western cape (multi-region)  

**Holden Manz Wine Estate**  
22ha at the top, southernmost corner of the valley between the Franschhoek River and the Stony Brook at ~300m; red-only grower — just the top 16ha planted (Cab, Cab Franc, Syrah, Merlot), three soil types.  
People: Gerard Holden & Migo Manz (founders, 1990s)  

**La Bri**  
One of the original nine Huguenot farms: granted October 1694 to Jacques (Jacob) de Villiers.  
People: Robin Hamilton (owner since 1997); winemaker/manager Irene Waller (née de Fleuriot) since 2006.  

**La Chataigne Wines**  
Boutique; own bottling since 2003; vineyards back to 1942.  

**La Couronne Wines**  
Named for LA Couronne, the 68-gun French warship (built 1636 under Richelieu's naval renewal) that carried Huguenot refugees to the Cape.  

**La Motte**  
1695 grant to German immigrant Hans Hendrik Hattingh; bought 1709 by Huguenot Pierre Joubert and named for his birthplace La Motte d'Aigues (Provence); first 4,000 vines planted 1752 by Gabriël du Toit.
