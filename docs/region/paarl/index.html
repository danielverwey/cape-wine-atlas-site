HOW TO READ THIS PAGE

NOTHING IS WRITTEN HERE WITHOUT A SOURCE. Every honour links to the page where it was found. Our researchers may only record what they can cite; an entry is promoted to “confirmed” only once a second, independent reviewer has gone back to the source.

“NOT YET RESEARCHED” IS NOT “NO AWARDS”. The atlas is careful to tell “nobody has looked yet” apart from “nothing was won” — many fine producers simply never enter competitions.

WHAT IS HELD BACK. Where a detail rests on a source the atlas is not free to republish, it is withheld until the producer’s own site or an open record confirms it. Claims still under review are counted, never presented as fact.

The Cape Wine Atlas is an open record of South Africa’s wine farms, gathered one estate at a time from the producers’ own pages, the wine routes’ own rosters and the competitions’ own results. Nothing in it was written from memory: every entry points to the place it was found, every honour to the page that announced it, and where the research has not yet reached, the atlas says so rather than pretending.

The data is meant to be free — to be read, questioned, corrected and reused. This page is the atlas rendered as a work of art: the valleys drawn in light, the estates as signals on a dark chart, the record itself as something worth looking at as well as looking up.
